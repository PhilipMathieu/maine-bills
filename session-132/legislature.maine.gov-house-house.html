Welcome to the Maine House of Representatives

The Maine House of Representatives is committed to serving the people of Maine through transparent, accessible government. Here you'll find information about your elected representatives, legislative documents, committee activities, and opportunities to engage with the democratic process.

132nd Legislature: The House currently has 151 active members consisting of 75 Democrats, 73 Republicans, 1 Independent, 2 Unenrolled,and 2 Tribal Representatives.

Whether you're tracking legislation, planning a visit to the State House, or seeking to contact your representative, this website provides the tools and information you need to stay informed and involved in Maine's legislative process.

What is SINE DIE?
"Sine die" is a Latin term meaning "without day" - it indicates that the legislative session has adjourned with no appointed day to reconvene.
